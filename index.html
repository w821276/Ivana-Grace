Get to Know Ivana Grace

Ivana Grace is an eclectic soul vocalist, singer-songwriter, spoken word artist, and singing doula whose work blends Soul, Funk, Jazz, Neo-Soul, Gospel, Reggae, and R&B into unforgettable musical experiences. More than a performance, every Ivana Grace show is a journey. Through rich harmonies, heartfelt storytelling, and rhythms that move both body and spirit, she creates spaces where people can connect, heal, celebrate, and feel deeply. Whether performing at festivals, weddings, private events, community gatherings, corporate functions, or intimate listening rooms, Ivana delivers authentic artistry, powerful vocals, and a soulful presence that lingers long after the music ends. As a singing doula, Ivana is committed to supporting families while helping bring awareness to the maternal health crisis through advocacy, education, and the healing power of music. Ivana Grace is a genre-blended experience where music meets purpose, storytelling meets healing, and every performance becomes a memorable connection.
